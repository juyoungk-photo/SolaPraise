SolaPraise

A free, open-source app for a church worship team · iPhone and iPad

SolaPraise brings three things a worship team does into one place: finding and playing 찬양, following the daily 말씀, and preparing the 예배 together.

What it does

  • 찬양 — worship feeds from the channels your team follows, search, playlists, and on-device chord detection that turns a recording into a lead sheet you can transpose.
  • 말씀 — the day's QT and sermon videos, with the passage they preach one tap away, plus a Psalms reader.
  • 예배 — the service plan, roles and sign-ups, backed by a Google Sheet your team already owns.

Privacy

There is no backend and no account with this project. Nothing is collected or transmitted to anyone operating it, because there is nobody operating it — the app talks to Google's APIs directly from your device. Sign-in is optional, the tokens live in your device's keychain, and audio you analyse never leaves the device.
